What is new in this build

- Discover tab in Browse: picks from AniList (rising, new in the top 50, completed, classics, hidden gems, older and still running), matched to your pinned sources. Refreshes once a day on Wi-Fi, shows a status line while it works, and fills in as titles are found.
- Rows open up to 50 titles with "See all". Titles already in your library or already read are left out. Long press a card for "Not interested".
- New Discover rows: "Because you read X" (what readers of your recent reads went on to read), "Your genres" (any of your top genres) and "Fresh starts". Cards that were not in yesterday's lists are tagged "New today".
- New row "My genres": you pick the genres yourself (Discover > My genres > Choose genres, several at once, any of them counts; titles matching more of your genres rank higher). It shows your top 30 with "See all", stays the same all day, and is ranked again the next day or as soon as you change the genres.
- Reader: tap scroll distance and time are settings (Reader settings > General). Pages that lose their image while on screen ask for it again by themselves.
- Release file names carry the version number, so downloads of different builds do not collide.
- Auto scroll asks the screen for its highest refresh rate while it runs, to avoid dropping to 60 Hz and looking choppy.
- Enhancement settings show a "Last auto scroll" line with frame timings (frames, fps, skipped refreshes, slow frames), to trace stutter during auto scroll.
- "My genres" can also use Mecha, Music and Adult (18+) (AniList's adult genre). Adult only shows up when you pick it yourself.
- Auto scroll: the page list now tells Android its content moves fast, so the screen should stay at its highest refresh rate instead of dropping to half (the "Last auto scroll" line shows the result).
- Auto scroll: a tiny invisible surface now asks the screen for its refresh rate directly (experiment, ColorOS may revise window-level requests).
- Reader settings > General: a checkbox turns the tiny auto scroll refresh-rate dot on or off.
